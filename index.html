About Us

We’re a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to giving young people their first taste of aviation.

Albert Whitted Flying Club brings together families and volunteers who love flying. Our mission is to inspire the next generation of pilots and aviation enthusiasts through hands-on experiences, mentorship, and a shared passion for the sky. Whether it’s learning about aircraft, visiting the airport, or taking to the air, we’re here to make flying accessible and fun for kids.
